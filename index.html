Questions

Good to know.

Which batch should I join?

Join the batch for the year you sit your A/L: the 2027 batch or the 2028 batch. If you're already in your exam year, the Crash Course is built for final revision. Not sure? Use "Find your class" above, or just ask Leon.

Do you teach online?

Yes. Every batch runs live online alongside the in-person classes at Nugegoda and Kiribathgoda, so you can join from anywhere.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the batch. Send an enquiry and Leon will share the current fees and everything you need to get started.

I'm just starting out. Can I still join?

Absolutely. The 2028 batch is ideal for a head start, and the Crash Course is there for exam-year students who need focused revision.

Where are the classes held?

In person at Nugegoda and Kiribathgoda, plus live online. Enquire for the current class days and times at each location.
